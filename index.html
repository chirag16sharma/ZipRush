Rules

Play fair

  1. Trace one continuous path that visits every cell in the grid.
  2. Hit the numbered checkpoints in order, starting at 1 and finishing on the highest number.
  3. Move up, down, left, or right only, and never cross a wall.
  4. Each cell can be visited only once.
  5. A hint adds the next correct cell and is counted against your result.
Tip: look for the next number before you move. A wrong move won't count.
